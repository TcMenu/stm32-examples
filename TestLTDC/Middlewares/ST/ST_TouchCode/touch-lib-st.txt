This code is taken from the official ST discovery Library. I've modified it
to work with the STM32F4Discovery board. It exposes a few functions for mapping
the touchscreen to the STM32F4Discovery board.

https://github.com/STMicroelectronics/x-cube-azrtos-f4/tree/befedcdd72388c9c9305eccf5208a19845c31b47/Drivers/BSP/Components/Common

and

https://github.com/STMicroelectronics/32f429idiscovery-bsp/tree/main

I'm not even going to say this is the right way to do things, but for the sake of example it works fairly
well, and if you're trying to use the same touchscreen in your project then this may be a good starting
 point.
